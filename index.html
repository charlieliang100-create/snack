Ingredients

What's inside

Doritos Nacho Cheese

Corn chips made with corn, canola oil, maltodextrin, whey powder, salt, sugar, cream powder, spices, tomato powder, onion powder, yeast extract, colors, flavors, and food acids. Contains milk, soy, and gluten ingredients.

Takis Fuego

Rolled chips made with precooked maize flour, sunflower or maize oil, maltodextrin, citric acid, salt, flavoring, starch, flavor enhancers, sugar, vegetable concentrates, paprika extract, and lemon-lime essential oil. May contain soy, milk, and gluten.

Chips Ahoy! Original

Cookies made with enriched wheat flour, semisweet chocolate chips, sugar, soybean or canola oil, palm oil, high fructose corn syrup, leavening, salt, flavors, and caramel color. Contains milk, wheat, and soy.

Little Debbie Cosmic Brownie

Brownie made with enriched wheat flour, corn syrup, palm and soybean oils, sugar, dextrose, water, cocoa, candy pieces, soy lecithin, salt, corn starch, leavening, caramel color, flavors, eggs, whey, and soy flour. Contains wheat, soy, milk, and egg.

Nature Valley Oats 'n Honey

Crunchy granola bars made with whole grain oats, sugar, canola or sunflower oil, rice flour, honey, salt, brown sugar syrup, baking soda, soy lecithin, and natural flavor. Contains soy; may contain peanut, almond, and pecan ingredients.

Welch's Mixed Fruit Snacks

Fruit snacks made with whole fruit puree, corn syrup, sugar, modified corn starch, gelatin, grape juice concentrate, citric and lactic acids, flavors, vitamins A, C, and E, carnauba wax, and colors from annatto, turmeric, and fruit or vegetable juice.

Bottled Water

Water only. Check the bottle label if Mia changes brands.

Capri Sun Fruit Punch

Juice drink made with filtered water, sugar, pear, grape, orange, apple, and pineapple juice concentrates, citric acid, monk fruit concentrate, natural flavor, and mushroom extract to protect quality.
